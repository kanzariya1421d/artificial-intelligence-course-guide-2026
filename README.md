What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines programmed to think like humans and mimic their actions. The goal of AI is to enable computers to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as visual perception, speech recognition, decision-making, and language translation. With various applications ranging from self-driving cars to intelligent personal assistants, AI has transcended theoretical research and become a pivotal part of contemporary technology.
